# 03 Lab: Ocean Survival Mechanics

## Lab Objective
Implement swimming physics, oxygen depletion meter, water buoyancy, and raft interaction mechanics.

## Step-by-Step Build
1. Connect WaterBodyOcean with Physics Volumes.
2. Add WaterBuoyancyComponent to dynamic raft meshes.
3. Handle player immersion state in Character Movement Component (SetMovementMode(MOVE_Swimming)).
